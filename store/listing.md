## Long description

```
InfoCord is a private notes app built around end-to-end encryption. Your note content is encrypted on your device before it ever reaches our servers — we cannot read it.

• End-to-end encryption — AES-GCM with keys derived from your password (PBKDF2, 310,000 iterations)
• Zero-knowledge server — we store ciphertext only; decryption happens on your device
• Sync across web and mobile — sign in on any device with the same account
• Categories and organization — keep notes structured without sacrificing privacy
• Account deletion — permanently remove your account and data from Settings

InfoCord is designed for people who want the convenience of cloud sync without giving up control of their personal writing, research, and planning.

Note content is never used for advertising or sold to third parties. See our privacy policy for full details on what we collect (account email, session metadata) versus what we cannot access (your note plaintext).

Requires an internet connection. Offline sync is planned for a future release.
```
