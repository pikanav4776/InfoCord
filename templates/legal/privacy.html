InfoCord Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Contact: support@infocord.app (placeholder)

Summary

InfoCord is an end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) note app. We cannot read your note content. Notes are encrypted on your device before they reach our servers.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, and a hashed password (we never store your plaintext password).
  • Encrypted note data: ciphertext, initialization vectors (IV), and per-note salt metadata. This is unreadable without your password.
  • Technical logs: request timestamps, HTTP method, path, status code, and a random request ID. We do not log note content.

What we do not collect

  • Plaintext note content
  • Your encryption keys or recovery keys (recovery keys are shown once at signup; only a hash is stored)
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics tracking pixels

How encryption works

Your password derives an encryption key on your device using PBKDF2 and AES-GCM. If you lose your password and did not save your recovery key, we cannot recover your notes.

Third-party services

  • Render — hosts the API
  • Neon — hosts the PostgreSQL database (encrypted note blobs and account metadata)

Data retention & deletion

You may delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion permanently removes your account, notes, categories, and authentication tokens from our database.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. CCPA in California, DPDPA in India), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because note content is encrypted with keys we do not hold, we can only delete encrypted blobs — not decrypt them for export.

Changes

We will update this policy when practices change. Material changes will be communicated in-app or via email.
